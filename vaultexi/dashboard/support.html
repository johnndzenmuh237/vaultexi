Quick answers

How long does a loan decision take?
Applications are reviewed within a maximum of 24 hours. Your Loan page shows Under review with a countdown, then the decision and a message from our team. After approval your account is credited within the next 24 hours.
Why is my transfer pending?
Every transfer is reviewed before it is sent. The amount is deducted from your loan balance only after approval. If it is rejected, nothing is deducted and the message in your history explains what to do next.
Why was my transfer rejected?
The most common reasons are incorrect bank details or missing verification. Open Transfer, then Transaction history, and read the message on that transfer. Fix what it asks for and submit again.
When can I access my security deposit?
Coins in your security balance stay locked. Your security deposit is accessible only after your loan amount is fully repaid.
